### Standard SOP Core Sections (Formatted Style)

## 1.0 PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE

To standardize the cultivation process of tomatoes and eggplants from seedling management to harvest, ensuring optimal fruit quality, high yield, and compliance with local agricultural biosafety standards.

## 2.0 SCOPE

This SOP applies to all field workers, farm supervisors, and agronomy technicians operating within the open-field production blocks and nursery green-houses of Fresco Greenovations.

## 3.0 ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Operational Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| *Farm Technicians* | Responsible for daily irrigation monitoring, scouting for pests, and executing weekly pruning. |
| *Farm Supervisor* | Responsible for verifying fertilizer mixing ratios, approving chemical interventions, and signing off on harvest readiness. |

## 4.0 DEFINITIONS & ACRONYMS

* *CRH:* Carbonized Rice Hull.
* *EFSB:* Eggplant Fruit and Shoot Borer (Leucinodes orbonalis).
* *Basal Application:* Applying fertilizer directly into the soil before or during planting.

## 5.0 MATERIALS, TOOLS, & EQUIPMENT

### 5.1 Inputs & Consumables

* Hybrid nightshade seeds (Tomato & Eggplant)
* Granular fertilizers (14-14-14 complete, 0-0-60 potash)
* Well-cured organic compost / Agricultural lime

### 5.2 Tools & Field Machinery

* 104-hole plastic seedling trays
* Silver-and-black protective plastic mulch
* Split bamboo stakes & horizontal nylon twine strings
* Sanitized bypass pruning shears & calibrated backpack sprayers

## 6.0 STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE

### Phase 1: Nursery Sowing & Seedling Management

1. *Mix* a propagation medium containing carbonized rice hull and rich organic compost.
2. *Sow* hybrid seeds thin and uniform across nursery trays at a depth of 0.5 cm.
3. *Provide* partial shade and structured rain shelters to prevent heavy downpour damage.
4. *Harden* all young seedlings one week before field transfer by reducing moisture intervals.

### Phase 2: Land Preparation & High-Bed Engineering

1. *Plow* and harrow production blocks twice to achieve an optimized soil structure.
2. *Form* raised beds exactly 1 meter wide, maintaining a height of 20 to 30 cm for drainage.
3. *Incorporate* baseline basal fertilizer mixes evenly into soil blocks before covering.
4. *Lay* silver-and-black plastic mulch film tightly across beds, anchoring outer perimeters.

### Phase 3: Field Transplanting & Crop Support

1. *Punch* clean planting holes spaced 50 cm apart using heated cutting tools.
2. *Transplant* tomatoes deeply up to their first true leaves; seat eggplants flush with the surface.
3. *Install* a bamboo Florida-weave trellis framework along rows to safeguard against wind lodging.
4. *Pinch* out vegetative lateral axil suckers weekly on vining tomatoes to isolate single main trunks.

## 7.0 QUALITY CONTROL & TROUBLESHOOTING

| If This Happens (Problem) | The Root Cause Is Likely | Action Required (Fix) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seedlings look yellow and spindly | Low light or nitrogen deficiency | Move to full sun; apply low-dose liquid fertilizer. |
| Tomato fruit bottoms turn black/leathery | Blossom-End Rot (Calcium issue / uneven watering) | Stabilize watering schedule; apply calcium side-dress. |
| Sudden whole-plant wilting during noon | Bacterial Wilt (Ralstonia) | *Do not water.* Pull plant immediately, burn it, lime the hole. |

## 8.0 HEALTH, SAFETY, & ENVIRONMENTAL GUARDRAILS

**WARNING:** Field hands must wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)—including rubber gloves and masks—whenever handling concentrated fertilizers or botanical pest sprays. Always sanitize pruning shears with a 10% bleach solution between rows to prevent cross-contamination of plant viruses.
